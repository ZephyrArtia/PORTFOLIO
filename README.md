---

## 👀 Interactive Banner Eye System
- **Pure Iris Cursor Tracking**: The glowing violet anime eyes follow your mouse cursor smoothly across the entire screen using spring-damped physics.
- **Natural Saccades & Idle Glances**: When the mouse is stationary, the eyes subtly glance in different directions like a living character.
- **No Blinking**: The eyes remain open and expressive at all times with the pupils fluidly tracking the cursor.
